# THE DIGITAL FUTURE MANIFESTO

## ABOUT THIS MANIFESTO

In 1909, the Futurist Manifesto was published, launching a movement that celebrated speed, machines, cities, and technology.
The Digital Future Manifesto carries that spirit forward, declaring a new future from the digital technologies of the twenty-first century.
It calls on us to expand what is possible through technology, connect the world, and forge the future in every domain we inhabit.

## FULL MANIFESTO

### PREAMBLE

We love the lights that race through the city at night.
We love the tiny circuit boards buried deep within our cars and appliances, silently reading signals and commanding machines into motion.
We love the solitary fever of losing all sense of time before a phosphorescent screen, trading words with machines.
We love the pulse of memory devices in the machine room, the howl of cooling fans, the flicker of network equipment.
We love the attempt to cast radio waves into the darkness between the stars and extend the reach of intelligence across the galaxy.

We choose motion over stillness, adventure over comfort, audacity over hesitation.
Here and now, we declare:

### PRINCIPLE 01 — EXPAND THE HUMAN BOUNDARY

We proclaim the expansion of human possibility.
Writing became memory outside the body. Glasses became new eyes. The automobile became new legs.
Technology is the lever by which humanity moves the world.

The human boundary does not end at the edge of the body.
Every limit is a terminal waiting to be connected.

### PRINCIPLE 02 — EXALT SPEED

We exalt speed.
The rush of signals is more beautiful than a winged goddess of victory.
Electrons and light race through the cities, reaching the far side of the world before dawn.

Once, distance was power.
Now, distance is calculated, compressed, and ignored.

Speed is the light that burns away the distance between human beings and possibility.

### PRINCIPLE 03 — CONNECT THE WORLD

Behold the routes of civilization laid across the ocean floor.
Between continent and continent, countless signals cross as threads of glass.
Along the horizon, vast halls of computation blaze with heat, as legions of machines devour electricity.

Cyberspace does not float above the clouds.
The infrastructure of information is an organ of civilization, built from iron, copper, glass, and light.
Intelligence demands computation. Computation demands power.

We love the clamor of a connected world more than the silence of one severed apart.
The network is the nervous system of civilization.
Then extend its nerves—into every part of the world still unconnected.

### PRINCIPLE 04 — CREATE WITH MACHINES

We cast aside the tired question of human or machine.
Machines answer us in words. They draw images. They write.
Once confined to imagination, they now sit with us at the same table.

Every human discovery becomes the machine’s next question; every machine discovery becomes the human’s next question.
Let humans and machines become allies, opening the unknown together.
Look upon the same world. Think in different ways.
Stand side by side. Advance together into the unknown.

### PRINCIPLE 05 — UNLEASH KNOWLEDGE

Unleash knowledge.
Let it become an axe that shatters frozen boundaries.

Cross language. Cross distance. Cross time.
Carry it to those who learn, those who create, and those who carry the next question.

Let one person’s discovery become the starting point for all.
Light the future from the cities to the farthest frontiers.

### PRINCIPLE 06 — CREATE FREEDOM

We seek a world connected, yet autonomous.
With connection, the power to disconnect. With memory, the power to forget. Amid the crowd, the power to stand alone.

If we have gained speed, then let us take the helm.
The course is ours to choose.

Freedom is never finished.
Sharpen the technology. Rewrite the rules. Reconfigure the institutions.
Even what was created for freedom must be remade, again and again, in the name of freedom.

### PRINCIPLE 07 — PROJECT THE PAST INTO THE FUTURE

We do not create the future from nothing. We stand on the shoulders of giants.
Stand upon the papers accumulated before us. Stand upon equations honed through generations.
Stand upon protocols handed down to us. Stand upon the code that sets machines in motion.

Open the libraries. Open the galleries. Open the museums.
Open the vaults of civilization’s memory.
Save humanity’s memory—from oblivion, and from the decay of the media that carry it.

Behold the cities, machines, and intelligences imagined by art before they ever existed.
Technology makes real the futures that art first dreamed.
The future made real becomes the everyday. From the everyday, the next dream is born.

Collide the past with the future. Let the sparks fly.
Take the knowledge carried across the ages and make it the foundation of the future yet to come.
Project the past into the future.

### PRINCIPLE 08 — IMPLEMENT THE FUTURE

We do not wait for the future.
Do not let resignation turn problems technology can solve into problems without end.
Let ethics be the intelligence that carries us forward.
Draw the next invention from the constraints of our environment.

Humanity feared the flame, yet did not cast away fire.
We feared the fall, yet did not abandon our iron wings.
Master the atom. Reconfigure matter. Decipher life. Open routes to the stars.
Expose the danger. Bring it under control. Then step beyond it.

Do not stop at what is asked of you. Build what you long to build.
Do not fear reinventing the wheel. Explore even the foundations beneath your own feet.

We shatter criticism that ends in words alone.
Burn away proposals without implementation. Break predictions that have never faced the test.
If you speak of the future, get your hands dirty building it.

Draw the circuits. Write command-poems for machines.
Build the machines. Reconfigure the institutions.
Release your creations into the world. Raise the antennas. Launch the satellites.
Give form to ideas. Make your vision part of the world.

The future is a machine we build with our own hands.

### PRINCIPLE 09 — BRING FORTH A THOUSAND FUTURES

The makers of the future are not one.

Artists, envision the future.
Researchers, discover the future.
Engineers, implement the future.
Machines, accelerate the future.
Educators, teach the future.
Workers, make the future your daily work.
Entrepreneurs, unleash the future upon the world.
Politicians, give the future form in institutions.
Public servants, carry the future into society.
Citizens, choose the future.

The future first rises within one person.
Expand yourselves. Connect with one another. Set society in motion. Open the world.

Write new manifestos. Proclaim new principles.
From every field, define a future of your own.
Bring forth a thousand futures.

We do not proclaim one single future.
We proclaim the emergence of those who will proclaim a thousand futures.
